◆ [ Scope boundaries ] What is included
  • Website development is limited to the agreed pages, the CMS setup and the website features listed above.
  • Brand deck design is capped at 10–12 slides. Client supplies final copy, facts, images and data.
  • Up to two consolidated revision rounds are included for the agreed work. Additional revisions or new directions may be quoted separately.
  • Full event production graphics (stage backdrops, signage, badges), full press-kit production, multiple social campaigns, research-report layouts, motion/video and ongoing content management are excluded.
  • Domain registration, hosting, paid third-party services and subscriptions, and other external costs are excluded from this fee and are borne directly by the client.
